What this setup does:

- Installs Hatchling and its bundled Room image into your prepared hatchling-wsl distro.
- Adds Hatchling to your Start menu.
- If you choose, keeps it running while you're logged in.

Updating? Your settings, model and data stay as they are. Hatchling restarts, so anything it's working on stops.

It needs no administrator rights. Your PINs, keys and codes are only ever typed at Hatchling's own prompt, and this setup never sees them.
