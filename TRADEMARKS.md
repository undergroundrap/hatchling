# The Hatchling name and logo

Hatchling's code is open source, and you're welcome to fork it, change it and build on it under its license. The name and the logo are how people know the original project, so they work differently. This page says, in plain language, what you can and can't do with them. The binding version is term 3 of [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md).

## You can

- Say your project is **"based on Hatchling"**, **"a fork of Hatchling"** or **"built on Hatchling"**.
- Name Hatchling when you describe, review, compare, teach or write about it.
- Use the name in a package, plugin or integration that works *with* Hatchling, when its name makes clear it isn't Hatchling itself: `hatchling-plugin-foo` from you, say.
- Show the logo when you're writing about Hatchling itself, unaltered.

## You can't

- Call your fork or product **"Hatchling"**, or give it a name that could be mistaken for it.
- Present a modified version as the official Hatchling, an official release, or something made or endorsed by Ocean Bennett.
- Use the Hatchling logo, or one confusingly like it, as your own project's or product's logo.

Forks must keep the attribution in [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md): "Built on Hatchling by Ocean Bennett".

## Questions

If you're not sure whether a use is fine, ask: <https://oceanbennett.com/hatchling/>. Most reasonable uses are.
